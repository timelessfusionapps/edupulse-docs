It is a modern school engagement and rewards platform focused on:
- student motivation
- house competitions
- leaderboard systems
- gamification
- school culture
- participation tracking
- event engagement
- recognition systems

The dashboard should feel:
- modern
- energetic
- motivating
- rewarding
- premium
- emotionally engaging
- clean
- highly usable

- accounting software
