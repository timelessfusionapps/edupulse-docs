# 1. Purpose

## 1.1 Governance Objective

This document defines governance rules for:

- Student Clubs
- Student Council
- House Prefects
- Advanced Leadership

including:

- Assignment Rules
- Membership Rules
- Capacity Rules
- Lifecycle Rules
- Historical Preservation Rules

The objective is to provide structure and accountability while preserving EduPulse's core mission:

```text
Student Participation
↓
Events
↓
Points
↓
House Competition
↓
Recognition
```

---

# 2. Governance Principles

## 2.1 Historical Preservation First

EduPulse follows:

```text
History Never Replaced
History Always Preserved
```

All appointments, memberships and assignments must remain historically visible.

---

## 2.2 Academic-Year Governance

Leadership and council assignments belong to a specific academic year.

No leadership assignment is permanent.

Every academic year begins with fresh appointments.

---

## 2.3 Platform Simplicity Principle

The platform intentionally avoids:

- Elections
- Voting
- Campaigns
- Approval Chains

Appointments are administrative decisions.

---

# 3. Club Governance

## 3.1 Club Ownership

A Club belongs to a School.

Examples:

- Science Club
- Robotics Club
- Eco Club
- Literary Club

Clubs are permanent organizational entities.

---

## 3.2 Club Creation Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may create Clubs.

Teachers may not create Clubs.

---

## 3.3 Club Modification Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may:

- Rename Clubs
- Archive Clubs
- Modify Club Categories

---

## 3.4 Club Coordinators

A Club may have:

- One Coordinator
- Multiple Coordinators

Coordinator assignments are teacher-based.

---

## 3.5 Coordinator Assignment Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may assign Club Coordinators.

---

## 3.6 Club Membership

Students may:

- Join Clubs
- Leave Clubs
- Rejoin Clubs

during the same academic year.

Multiple club memberships are permitted.

---

## 3.7 Club Membership History

Every membership action must create a historical record.

Examples:

```text
Joined Club

Left Club

Rejoined Club
```

Membership history must never be deleted.

---

# 4. Student Council Governance

## 4.1 Council Structure

EduPulse supports:

```text
Student Council
+
Prefects
```

as the official governance structure.

---

## 4.2 Council Membership Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may appoint Student Council Members.

---

## 4.3 Council Capacity

Student Council membership is unlimited.

The platform imposes no numerical restriction.

Schools determine operational requirements.

---

## 4.4 Council History

Historical records must preserve:

- Academic Year
- Student
- Assignment Date
- Removal Date

Historical records are immutable.

---

# 5. Advanced Leadership Governance

## 5.1 Supported Positions

The following positions are supported:

- Head Boy
- Head Girl
- Sports Captain

---

## 5.2 Assignment Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may assign leadership positions.

---

## 5.3 Position Capacity Rules

The platform enforces:

### Unique Positions

```text
1 Head Boy

1 Head Girl

1 Sports Captain
```

per academic year.

Duplicate appointments are prohibited.

---

## 5.4 Multiple Leadership Assignments

A student may simultaneously hold:

- Club Memberships
- Council Membership
- House Leadership
- Advanced Leadership

provided position capacity rules remain satisfied.

---

## 5.5 Leadership History

Every appointment must preserve:

- Academic Year
- Student
- Position
- Appointment Date
- End Date

Historical appointments may not be overwritten.

---

# 6. House Prefect Governance

## 6.1 House-Specific Model

Prefects are House-specific.

Examples:

```text
Blue House Prefect

Green House Prefect

Yellow House Prefect

Red House Prefect
```

---

## 6.2 Appointment Authority

Only:

- School Admin
- Principal

may appoint Prefects.

---

## 6.3 Prefect Capacity

Multiple Prefects may exist within a House.

No platform capacity limit exists.

---

## 6.4 Prefect History

Historical records must preserve:

- House
- Student
- Academic Year
- Appointment Date
- End Date

---

# 7. Automatic Lifecycle Governance

## 7.1 Academic Year Expiry

At academic year completion:

- Leadership positions expire
- Council assignments expire
- Prefect assignments expire

Historical records remain preserved.

---

## 7.2 Student Lifecycle Events

Leadership assignments automatically terminate when:

- Student Graduates
- Student Transfers
- Student Becomes Inactive
- Student Is Archived

---

## 7.3 Club Membership Lifecycle

Club membership remains independent of leadership status.

A student may lose leadership while retaining club membership.

---

# 8. Deletion Governance

## 8.1 Hard Delete Prohibition

The following records may never be hard deleted:

- Clubs
- Club Membership History
- Coordinator History
- Council History
- Leadership History
- Prefect History

---

## 8.2 Archive Strategy

EduPulse uses:

```text
Soft Delete
+
Archive
```

for all Phase 2H entities.

---

# 9. Notification Governance

## 9.1 Future Notification Integration

Future integration with Phase 2F may permit:

- Club Announcements
- Council Announcements
- Leadership Announcements

Phase 2H itself does not own notification delivery.

---

# 10. Points Governance Protection

## 10.1 Phase 2E Authority

The following remain exclusively governed by Phase 2E:

- Points
- Achievements
- Recognition
- Rankings
- Leaderboards

---

## 10.2 Club Competition Prohibition

Phase 2H shall not create:

- Club Points
- Club Rankings
- Club Awards
- Club Achievement Systems

without a future approved architecture revision.

# 12. Governance Verdict

Phase 2H governance establishes:

- Clubs
- Student Council
- House Prefects
- Advanced Leadership

while preserving:

- House Governance
- Event Governance
- Points Governance
- Recognition Governance

The governance model remains intentionally lightweight, historically auditable, and aligned with EduPulse's core mission of participation, events, houses, points, and recognition.
