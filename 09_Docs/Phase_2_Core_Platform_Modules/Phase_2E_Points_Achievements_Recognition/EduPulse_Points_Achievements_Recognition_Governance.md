# Phase 2E — Points, Achievements & Recognition Governance

Version: 1.0
Status: Governance Approved
Phase: 2E

---

## 1. Point Ledger Governance

Every point action creates a permanent ledger transaction.

Transactions cannot be:

- Edited
- Deleted

Corrections require:

Correction Transaction

with mandatory reason.

---

## 2. Award Governance

Manual Awards:

Immediate assignment permitted.

Reason required.

Approval optional and configurable.

---

## 3. Deduction Governance

Manual Deductions:

Approval required.

Reason mandatory.

Bulk deductions supported.

Approval may occur:

- Individually
- As a batch

depending on workflow configuration.

---

## 4. Approval Governance

Approval roles are configurable.

No hardcoded role names permitted.

Approvals are permission-driven.

---

## 5. Achievement Governance

Achievements:

- Never expire
- Remain permanent
- Archive after Academic Year closure

Achievements may award bonus points.

Bonus values are configurable.

---

## 6. Badge Governance

Badges may be:

- Manual
- Automatic

Expiry modes:

- End of Term
- End of Academic Year
- Custom Date
- Never

Configured by School Admin.

---

## 7. Recognition Governance

Recognition Records:

- May contain points
- May contain achievements
- May exist independently

Recognition Records remain permanently archived.

---

## 8. Leaderboard Governance

Leaderboards must update automatically.

Offline synchronization is supported.

Recalculation must occur after synchronization.

---

## 9. Snapshot Governance

Academic Year closure must generate:

- Student Snapshot
- Class Snapshot
- House Snapshot

Snapshots are immutable.

---

## 10. Champion Governance

Version 1:

House Champion =
Highest House Points

Class Champion =
Highest Class Points

Custom formulas are deferred.

---

## 11. Visibility Governance

Supported Flags:

- studentVisible
- parentVisible
- teacherVisible

Future modules must respect these flags.

---

## 12. Bulk Governance

Bulk assignment must support:

- Preview
- Validation
- Audit Logging

No silent execution permitted.

---

## 13. Audit Governance

Audit Events:

- PointsAwarded
- PointsDeducted
- PointsCorrected
- AchievementAssigned
- BadgeAssigned
- BadgeExpired
- RecognitionAssigned
- LeaderboardGenerated
- SnapshotCreated
- ApprovalGranted
- ApprovalRejected

Audit records are immutable.

---

## 14. Academic Year Governance

At Year Closure:

Archive:

- Point Ledgers
- Achievements
- Badges
- Recognition Records
- Leaderboards

Create historical snapshots.

Reset leaderboard totals for the new year.

Historical records remain accessible.

---

## 15. Parent Portal Governance

Phase 2E provides metadata only.

No Parent Portal UI.

Future modules consume visibility metadata.

---

## 16. Future Enhancements

Deferred beyond Phase 2E:

- Custom Championship Formula Engine
- Certificate Generation Engine
- Achievement Recommendation Engine
- Gamification Missions
- Reward Redemption System
- Parent Recognition Dashboard
- AI Recognition Insights
