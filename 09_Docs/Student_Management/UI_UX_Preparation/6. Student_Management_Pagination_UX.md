## Purpose
Establishes an intuitive, scalable approach to navigating thousands of records.

## Infinite Scroll vs Standard Pagination
- **Mobile UX**: Infinite scrolling is vastly superior on mobile. As the user scrolls near the bottom of the `ListView`, a new chunk is fetched and appended.
- **Desktop UX**: Standard pagination controls (Prev, Next, Page X) are better for tables, but since Firestore cursor pagination (using `startAfter`) doesn't naturally support jumping to Page 10, we will use a "Load More" button at the bottom of the table or an Infinite Scroll implementation inside the table body itself.

## Loading State
Appending data should never replace the current list with a full loading skeleton. Instead, a small `CircularProgressIndicator` appears at the bottom of the list while the next chunk is appended seamlessly.
