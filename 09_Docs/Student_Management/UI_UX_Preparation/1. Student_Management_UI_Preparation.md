## Purpose
Establishes the foundational UX philosophy for the Student Management module, setting the blueprint for all future EduPulse business modules (Teachers, Houses, Rewards, etc.).

## Operational SaaS UX Principles
- **Data Density vs. Readability**: SaaS dashboards must present maximum actionable data without overwhelming the user. We utilize progressive disclosure—showing key metrics (Points, Rank) upfront and detailed history behind a click.
- **Predictable Navigation**: The Master-Detail pattern will be standard. The master list persists filters and pagination state when navigating back from a detail view.
- **Action Proximity**: Actions like 'Edit', 'Archive', or 'Award Points' should be accessible via contextual menus on the row/card to minimize cursor travel.
- **Zero Layout Shifts**: Heights and widths of containers (like tables or image placeholders) must be fixed or bounded to prevent Cumulative Layout Shifts (CLS) during realtime updates.
