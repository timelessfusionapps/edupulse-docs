## Purpose
Details the exact conditional rendering logic swapping complex Tables for mobile-friendly Cards.

## Desktop Strategy (Data Table)
- **Component**: Flutter's `PaginatedDataTable` or a custom `ListView` mimicking a table layout for finer control.
- **UX**: High information density. Sortable headers (client-side sorting on the current paginated chunk). Checkboxes for bulk actions (future scope).
- **Columns**: Avatar, Full Name, Admission No, Grade & Section, House, Total Points, Actions.

## Mobile Strategy (List of Cards)
- **Component**: `ListView.builder` containing `StudentCard` widgets.
- **UX**: Touch-friendly, vertically scrolling. 
- **Card Layout**:
  - *Leading*: Circular Avatar (Initials or Photo).
  - *Title*: Full Name.
  - *Subtitle*: Grade, Section, and House badge.
  - *Trailing*: Total Points indicator.
- **Transition Threshold**: LayoutBuilder will detect `maxWidth < 600`. Below this, the UI swaps entirely, disposing of the table controllers safely.
